Digital Product Ideas You Can Make for Free

A digital product is made once and sold many times, so it fits a zero budget. Narrow beats broad: "invoice kit for freelance photographers" sells better than "business templates".

Ideas that work with free tools

  • Spreadsheet templates (Google Sheets): budgets for a specific group, rental trackers, wedding planners.
  • Printable planners and checklists (Canva free plan).
  • Notion templates for students, freelancers or small teams.
  • Short how-to guides as PDFs, about something you know well.
  • Contract, proposal and email templates for freelancers. Add a "not legal advice" line.

Rules to follow

  • Build from scratch; don't copy other sellers' templates.
  • Use only fonts and images licensed for commercial use.
  • If you used AI to help, follow the platform's disclosure rules.
