How to Get Your First 10 Customers Without Paying for Ads

Your first customers come from people, not algorithms. Each method below costs nothing but time.

1. Your own network, honestly

Tell people what you started and who it is for. Don't ask them to buy; ask who they know who needs it.

2. Be useful in communities

Pick two Reddit communities or Facebook groups where your buyers hang out. Answer questions in depth for a week before you mention your product, and read each group's rules on promotion.

3. Pinterest for visual and digital products

Pinterest works like a search engine. Pins can bring visitors for months, but expect a few weeks before traffic grows.

4. A free sample for an email

Offer a free mini version of your product in exchange for an email. Those subscribers are your warmest future buyers.

5. Ask every customer for feedback

The first ten buyers tell you what to fix and what to make next. A short thank-you email with one question is enough.

Stay legal

Don't send marketing emails or texts to people who did not agree to receive them. Many countries, Israel included, fine unsolicited commercial messages.
