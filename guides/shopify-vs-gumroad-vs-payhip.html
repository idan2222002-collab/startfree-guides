Shopify vs Gumroad vs Payhip: Which Should a Beginner Use?

All three let you sell online, but they suit different stages. Check each platform's current pricing page before you decide, since fees change.

Gumroad

Best for: your very first digital product. No monthly fee; Gumroad takes a percentage of each sale. Setup takes minutes, but you get little control over design and customer experience.

Payhip

Best for: digital products and courses with a bit more control. There is a free plan with a per-sale fee, and paid plans that lower it. It handles EU VAT for digital goods, which saves you headaches.

Shopify

Best for: a real brand, physical products, or many products. It has a monthly plan after the free trial, but you get a full store, your own domain, apps, inventory, shipping and point-of-sale. It is the platform most growing online shops end up on. Try Shopify free.

A simple rule

  • Testing one idea? Start on Gumroad or Payhip.
  • Selling every week, or adding physical products? Move to Shopify.
